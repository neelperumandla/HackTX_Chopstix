# Welcome to our HackTX 2024 project, codenamed *CHOPSTIX*
Our project features a silly, but useful robot.

For anyone with disabilities making meal time difficult,
~~or you're just plain lazy~~ our robot comes equipped with
a pair of chopsticks, and is capable of assisting people in
putting food in their mouth.

With the help of AI, the robot will recognize what food you
desire, properly pick up the food object, and gently place 
it in your mouth.

In future implementations, we plan on adding spoon
functionality so that the robot will pick up a spoon with
its chopsticks and use it to scoop up water/soup based
foods. Our prototype only works for certain foods right
now so future implementations could allow for it to work
with all types of foods.
